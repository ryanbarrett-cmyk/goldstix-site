How Goldstix protects payments

  • You approve every payment. Each payment needs Face ID, Touch ID or your device passcode on your own phone.
  • Codes can't redirect your money. A QR code, NFC tag or link only says who to pay. The app looks the recipient up on the Goldstix ledger and shows their name and goldtag before you approve.
  • Encrypted in transit. All traffic between the app and the ledger uses TLS.

Reporting a vulnerability

If you believe you've found a security problem in the Goldstix app, this website or the Goldstix ledger, email ryan@goldstix.com with "Security report" in the subject. Include the steps to reproduce it and the impact you observed.

  • We will acknowledge your report within 3 business days.
  • Please give us reasonable time to fix the problem before disclosing it publicly.
  • Test only with accounts you own. Don't access, change or delete other people's data, and don't degrade the service.
  • We will not pursue legal action over good-faith research that follows this policy.
