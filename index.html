How it works

Pay like cash. Count in gold.

The same moves you already know from payment apps, with one difference: the number on the screen is gold.

Tap to pay

Hold your phone to a Goldstix sticker at the counter, or to a friend's phone.

Scan a code

Every account has its own code. Scan it, check the name, and pay.

Get paid

Set an amount and show your code, or share a link. You'll see it land.

Face ID on every payment

Nothing leaves your account until you approve it on your own phone.

The Goldstix sign

One Goldstix is one thousandth of an ounce of gold.

A G with a stroke above and below, written before the number like a dollar sign. Amounts use two decimal places, so the smallest step is a hundredth of a Goldstix.
