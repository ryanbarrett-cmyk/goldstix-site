Privacy Policy DRAFT

Effective October 12, 2026 · Pending review by Goldstix Clearing compliance and counsel

This policy explains how Goldstix Clearing, LLC ("Goldstix", "we") handles information in the Goldstix mobile app and on goldstix.com. The app is currently in private testing.

Information we collect

Email address and passwordTo create your account and sign you in. Passwords are handled by Google Firebase Authentication; we never see or store them.
Name and goldtagTo show the people you pay, or who pay you, who they are dealing with.
PaymentsAmount, counterparty, memo, time and status of what you send and receive, to move Goldstix between accounts, show your activity, and keep ledger and settlement records.

Information that stays on your phone

  • Face ID, Touch ID or passcode. Used only to approve payments. Your phone's operating system handles biometric data; it never reaches us.
  • Camera. Used only to scan Goldstix QR codes. Images are not stored or sent to us.
  • NFC. Used only to read or write Goldstix payment tags and to tap phones. Only the payment code is processed.

What we don't do

  • We do not sell your information.
  • We do not use advertising, or third-party analytics or tracking, in the app or on this site.

Service providers

We use Google Firebase for account sign-in and cloud hosting providers to run the Goldstix ledger. They process information only to provide those services to us. The app also fetches the public gold spot price; that request contains no personal information.

Retention and your choices

We keep account and payment records while your account exists and for as long as financial recordkeeping rules require. To ask for a copy of your information, a correction, or deletion of your account, email ryan@goldstix.com.

Children

Goldstix is not intended for anyone under 18.

Changes

We will post any changes on this page with a new effective date.
